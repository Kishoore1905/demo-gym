FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. Your first visit includes a full session with a coach and access to any class that day. No card required.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most of our members started as beginners. Every new member gets an onboarding plan and technique coaching.

Can I freeze my membership?

You can freeze your membership for up to 60 days per year for travel or medical reasons.

Do you have parking and showers?

Yes — free parking for members, plus clean showers and secure lockers.
